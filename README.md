"# eversip-eco-friendly" 
EverSip – Eco-Friendly Water Bottle Marketing Campaign
🌱 About the Project
EverSip is an eco-friendly water bottle brand created to promote sustainable hydration and reduce reliance on single-use plastic bottles.

This project presents a complete promotional and marketing campaign for the EverSip brand, including visual branding, social media content, product information, client proposal, and professional communication materials.

🎯 Project Objectives
Create an attractive and memorable eco-friendly brand identity
Develop promotional content for multiple social media platforms
Present the product in a clear and conversion-focused way
Create engaging visuals and infographics
Prepare a professional client proposal with costing and schedule
Develop a professional email for client bidding
